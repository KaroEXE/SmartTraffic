Admin sign in

Enter the admin token. It is the value of NAV_ADMIN_TOKEN in backend/.env. It stays in this browser tab only and is cleared when you sign out or close the tab.

Overview

System status

In numbers

Driver reports

Reports sent from the apps (accidents, jams, closures, ...). Removing or resolving a report hides it from drivers.

Road features

Speed cameras, speed bumps, closures and roadworks that drivers see on the map. Only features you add here are shown: nothing is imported from elsewhere.

Places

Important places that you enter yourself (hospitals, mosques, landmarks and so on). Drivers see them on the map and find them when they search, before any result from the search provider. Names can be given in Arabic and Kurdish too.

Users

Accounts created in the mobile app. Passwords are never shown or stored in readable form.

Map

Active road features, driver reports and places. Click the map to add a road feature or a place at that point.
